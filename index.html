Who's this old man?

"Everybody's telling you how to get rich. I'm telling you how to stop getting poor."

Frank is an old rancher from the American heartland. He learned about money the hard way: bad loans, lean years, and a few purchases he still regrets. Then he watched his grandson Tyler make the very same mistakes, just with shinier trucks and more apps on his phone.

So Frank started talking. Every story he tells is about one money habit that quietly keeps people broke, and what he'd do instead.
